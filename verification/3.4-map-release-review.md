# Breuer GP 3.4 – Map final review

Production files are frozen. Exact source hashes are in `map-release-review.json`.

The world now uses a shared 16-cell painted facade atlas, four separately repeating painted ground surfaces, individual regional facade families/plinths, source-specific landmark surfaces and soft ground-contact/verge colour. Original streets, road layers, building footprints and measured roof data have identical SHA256 hashes. No geometry/physics budget thresholds were raised.

The low profile keeps the original wall triangle bands and five shared repeating painted materials. Tiled atlas walls and feathered ground overlays are rendered only in medium/high. The profiles never show both wall versions. The single extra peak low mesh is the red, source-referenced workshop floor material. Its terrain conformity was verified with the final native close-up.

## Verification

- `tests/scenery-identity.mjs`: sourced roof vertices, off-road garden bounds, bounded batches, deterministic identities passed.
- `tests/village-polish.mjs`: continuous terrain illumination, native sloped court rays and separate texture ownership passed after the final hall floor correction.
- `tests/road-surfaces.mjs`: 774 road rays and 12,395 oil rays across all three courses passed after the final hall floor correction.
- `tests/canvas-renderer.mjs`: real compatibility flow, original vertices/footprints, real items/oil, immutable race data and all five loaded car views passed.
- `tests/performance-production.mjs`: ten full world changes, six cars, all profiles, 30 extra quality transitions, stable resources and exact-once disposal passed; the final hall floor correction adds 16 visible native triangles. Root runs the final full chain.
- `native-textured-previews.json`: real decoded source WebP pixels, all packed high-wall triangles contained in one atlas cell, one shared high atlas, exclusive wall LODs, vertex-alpha fade below the real oil render order, and protected source hashes checked.

## Native image evidence

`native-breuer.png`, `native-workshop.png`, `native-school.png`, `native-bakery.png`, `native-roundabout.png`, `native-street.png` and `native-canvas-street.png` were rendered from the shipped native map/landmark meshes and actual local textures. The QA-only rasterizer is `render-native-world.mjs`, reused from the project's real vehicle artwork rasterizer, with outdoor lighting, vertex/instance colour and box-prefiltered diffuse mip approximations. No AI replacement scene is used.

The CPU views approximate filtering/lighting/transparency. They do not verify WebGL fog, shadowmaps, Three tone mapping or physical phone/PC FPS. Unnamed regional facades remain deterministic artistic interpretations; no fresh complete Street View survey is claimed.
